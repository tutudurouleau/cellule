    VANTAGE PRAGUE
    Vantage opened its Prague operation in 1998 to
    serve a busy market in features and commercials.
    Prague attracts a wide range of production business
    with a skilled workforce, attractive budgets and
    an outsized reputation and history in filmmaking.
    The ready availability of massive soundstages and
    dedicated production infrastructures makes Prague
    perfect for features, spots and television projects.
    Managing Director Jindřich Čipera oversees
    Vantage’s ten-person staff, which operates in a
    spacious, repurposed 1921 industrial building
    that includes 800 square meter of studio space,
    ten test bays, and plenty of parking on the roof.

     DEAR RENTAL CUSTOMER

     Over the course of three decades serving
     filmmakers, Vantage has kept the focus on
     cameras and lenses. This unique approach
     and the resulting expertise, along with
     invaluable input from filmmakers, is now the
     foundation of a worldwide company with
     offices in eight countries on three continents.
     Productions of every type and scale depend
     on Vantage’s vast inventory, precision lenses
     and proven logistics.

     Vantage strives to offer the highest quality
     and most sought-after equipment, exquisitely
     maintained and efficiently delivered. But
     Vantage’s ethos is to go the extra mile. The
     Vantage team includes many filmmakers
     with extensive set experience, starting with
     founders Peter Martin and Wolfgang Baumler,
     and the knowledge and understanding they
     bring to the operation goes beyond simply
     providing equipment. The network of Vantage
     offices makes each individual facility stronger,
     faster, and more reliable – more important
     than ever in today’s global film business.
     A deep understanding of what’s required
     informs the entire Vantage operation.


     Note: While Vantage carries a very wide
     array of equipment, we only carry tools
     we are convinced are the very best. If a
     particular desired item is not found in this
     catalog, we’re happy to obtain it for you.
     Our quality control procedures ensure
     flawless operation and will in many cases
     even improve performance.

     Your Vantage/Hawk Team

     HAWK HIDDEN CHAMPIONS

     Hidden Champion lenses are designed and built
     without compromise, setting a new standard in motion
     picture optics. Lenses in the new line will complement
     Hawk’s comprehensive array of existing lens lines.
     Every Hidden Champion is built upon Hawk’s 35 years
     of experience in motion picture lens calculation and
     manufacture. Hand-made in small numbers – and
     in some cases, unique – Hidden Champion lenses
     are freed from the compromises necessary for the
     uniform design and manufacture of full lens sets.
     They can be conceived, designed and built on much
     shorter timelines, without regard to costs, targeting
     very specific requirements. The project’s laboratory
     philosophy prioritizes responsiveness to the needs of
     cinematographers and openness to fresh discoveries –
     major advantages as the toolset continually evolves.
