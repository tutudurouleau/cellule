Hawk V-Lite Anamorphics –                                   Squeeze
                 As a part of its steady commitment to the film
                 industry Vantage has developed a new squeezing
                 ratio for its range of Hawk Anamorphics:
                 Hawk Anamorphic 1.3x SQUEEZE.

                 Hawk V-Lite 1.3x lenses can be used on all
                 modern digital and film cameras. The unique
                 squeezing factor of 1.30x makes it possible
                 to use nearly the entire sensor area of a
                 16:9 digital camera and achieve the popular
                 widescreen 1:2.40 release format. The new
                 Hawks compress the wider image to the size
                 of the smaller sensor. No top/bottom cropping
                 of the sensor area is required. This means
                 maximum image quality combined with a very
                 pleasant emotional effect.

                 Furthermore the new lenses allow shooting with
                 a 4:3 sensor and stretch it to 1:78 for 16:9
                 HDTV release. With an Arri Alexa 4:3 the full
                 sensor area can be used for 16:9 filming or
                 1:1.85 theatrical release.
