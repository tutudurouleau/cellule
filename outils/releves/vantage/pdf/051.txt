HAWK 1.3x SQUEEZE
FRONT ANAMORPHIC ZOOMS
Combine the unique characteristics of Hawk 1.3x
Squeeze Anamorphic Primes with the convenience of
zoom lenses.
Unlike existing anamorphic zooms, the Hawk Front
Anamorphic Zoom Lenses place the anamorphic element
in front of the spherical elements. The resulting image
displays all the anamorphic blur, shallow depth of
field, elliptical highlights, streaks and flares, geometric
curvature and barrel distortion, and distinct planes of focus
cinematographers expect from anamorphic primes.
Hawk 1.3x Squeeze Front Anamorphic Zooms deliver
excellent close focus ability, giving cinematographers
additional convenience and flexibility on the set.
