Import & Export Images (Overlay Freeze)
Individual frames captured in the Overlay-Menu can be exported. The original Images 1-3 are represented by the filenames
1_freeze, 2_freeze, and 3_freeze. To import any given image to the PSU Overlay-Menu, name these images 1_, 2_, and 3_
followed by any file name and upload them into the Overlay folder.

Job-Reports
The PSU automatically produces a detailed Job-Report for every shooting day. This PDF-document includes scene/take
number, length of the take, shooting time, take notes, camera speed, and one printed, original frame per scene. Press
NOTES >1sec. to display the keyboard. Press JOB REPORT to type the header of your production. The header will be quoted
automatically on every report. Job reports can be downloaded from the PSU as PDF files or added to a recorded DVD.
