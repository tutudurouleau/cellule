Vantage One
High performance T1 lenses - Technical Data
At T4, T2.8 and T2, Vantage One lenses can give cinematographers the flaw-
less look other modern lenses produce, but with superior image illumination.
And they offer additional versatility at other stops. At T1.4, the results are a bit
more forgiving, yet still extremely good, and at T1, Vantage One lenses lend the
image a lovely, subtle creaminess, perfect for skin tones.
