Vantage One4
Large Format Primes – Technical Data
The Vantage One4s contain no floating elements, no aspherics and
no expanders – only the best spherical elements. Vantage One4
lenses are original designs that deliver their full optical power to
new, larger formats, while also offering super close focus, compact
design, and mechanical robustness.
