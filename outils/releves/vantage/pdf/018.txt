Hawk V‑Lite16
1.3x Anamorphic Lenses - Technical Data
T1.5 high speed ‘Scope lenses for 16 mm. Hawk V‑Lite16 anamorphics are
smaller than most spherical lenses on the market. Still, they offer the same
stunning performance as all Hawk anamorphics. Finally, cinematographers
and camera operators have the opportunity to work with small S16 lenses that
are just as good as their 35 mm counterparts.
