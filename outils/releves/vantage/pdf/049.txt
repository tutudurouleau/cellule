Hawk V-Plus Vintage’74 Zooms
2x Front Anamorphic Zoom Lenses - Technical Data
Genuine Front Anamorphic Zoom Lenses. Hawk V‑Plus Vintage’74 front anamorphic zoom lenses deliver
lower contrast and flare characteristics of 1970s lenses with modern mechanics and the dependability,
sharpness and consistency of all current Hawk lenses. These zoom lenses intercut seamlessly with the
entire line of Hawk V‑Lite Vintage’74 and Hawk65 Vintage‘74 large format primes.
