HAWK C-SERIES ANAMORPHICS
For you it‘s an art, for us it‘s a science.


Hawk C-Series Anamorphics established Vantage’s
reputation for lenses with top-notch consistency and
durability. The very first lens line produced by Vantage
Film almost 25 years ago, the C-Series glass offers
timeless beauty and simplicity.

C-Series lenses deliver thoroughly modern and sharp
imagery that is also slightly forgiving, with a very natural
feel. Designed with an understanding of how filmmakers
work, the C-Series family includes a 60 mm/T 2.2 lens – a
very useful focal length in the 2x format that had usually
been left out in previous anamorphic lens sets. Because of
their popularity, Vantage continues to produce parts and
maintain the C-Series line.
