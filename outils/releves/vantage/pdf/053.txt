Hawk V-Plus 1.3x Zooms
Front Anamorphic Zoom Lenses - Technical Data
Genuine 1.3x front anamorphic zoom lenses. Unlike existing anamorphic zooms, the Hawk front anamorphic
zooms place the anamorphic element in front of the spherical elements. The resulting image displays all the
anamorphic blur, shallow depth of field, elliptical highlights, streaks and flares, geometric curvature and barrel
distortion, and distinct planes of focus. Hawk front anamorphic zoom lenses intercut seamlessly with our entire
line of 1.3x anamorphic primes.
