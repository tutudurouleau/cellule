Hawk V-Plus Zooms
2x Front Anamorphic Zoom Lenses - Technical Data
Unlike existing anamorphic zooms, the Hawk front anamorphic zoom lenses place the
anamorphic element in front of the spherical elements. The resulting image displays all the
anamorphic blur, shallow depth of field, elliptical highlights, streaks and flares, geometric
curvature and barrel distortion, and distinct planes of focus cinematographers expect from
anamorphic primes. Perfectly matched to Hawk V-Lite lenses.
