HAWK V-SERIES ANAMORPHICS
An extensive range of high performance scope lenses


The Hawk V-Series line of professional cine lenses was built
on a foundation of completely new optical calculations, with
the idea that cinematographers coming from the spherical
world would appreciate the precision of eleven choices in
focal length, rather than the four or five lenses in a standard
anamorphic set. The V-Series line extends superior lens quality
into very long focal lengths, including an unprecedented
250 mm lens. With two zooms and an accompanying 1.4x
extender, the V-Series has you covered.
At the other end of the scale, the
V-Series lenses deliver dramatic
breakthroughs in close
focus – down to two feet
in the middle range of
focal lengths. This virtue
is especially welcome for
cinematographers shooting
‘Scope in tight locations. The
V-Series lenses perform flawlessly
in strong backlight, with predictable
results and no flaring or halos.
