NEW ADDITION:
THE 140 MM T1.4
LARGE FORMAT LENS
The Vantage One4 line of large format spherical lenses
now includes a 140 mm-T1.4 lens, bringing additional
precision and flexibility to filmmakers. Vantage One4s
are designed from scratch with a unique philosophy that
produces distinctive lenses with superior close-focus.
All Vantage One4 lenses offer
a range of optical traits
depending on the stop.
The new 140 mm-T1.4
is surprisingly compact
despite a focal length
and T-stop requiring
large elements, and will
integrate flawlessly with
the Vantage One4 series.

The Vantage One4 140 mm
is mechanically robust, and
handcrafted with no floating
elements, no aspherics and no
expanders - only the highest
quality spherical optics.
