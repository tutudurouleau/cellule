Hawk V-Lite 1.3x
Anamorphic Lenses - Technical Data
Maximum image quality, combined with a unique emotional look. The unique squeeze
factor of 1.3x makes it possible to use nearly the entire sensor area of a 16:9 digital
camera to achieve the popular widescreen 2.40:1 release format. This means maximum
image quality combined with a very pleasant emotional effect. Furthermore, the 1.3x
lenses combined with a 4:3 sensor stretch the image to 1.78:1 for 16:9 TV release.
