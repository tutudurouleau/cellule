HAWK®
REPAIR AND
SPARE PARTS




Hawk lenses are designed and built to last forever,
and the same philosophy guides the Hawk service:
lens performance backed up by rock-solid support.
Hawk service technicians are the same experts
who build the lenses, which makes them uniquely
qualified to restore them to 100% performance. This
experience, plus a vast original parts inventory,
makes for ideal adjustment, refurbishment or
damage repair, usually undertaken at the Hawk
headquarters in Weiden, Germany where the lenses
were originally assembled,
or at our U.S. facility, Hawk
Anamorphic Los Angeles.

REPAIR SERVICE
• Before the repair of a lens, Hawk sends a
   detailed quotation, free of charge if the repair
   is executed. If the repair is declined, a €250
   charge is incurred.
• If additional work costing more than 10% of
   the estimate is found during the course of the
   repair, the repair is paused and the customer
   is notified.
• Repairs are done on state-of-the-art equipment
   that is build for or adapted to the specific
   purpose at Hawk headquarters, including an
   80-foot lens projection room.
• Express and overnight repairs are offered with
   additional charges.
• When timing is critical, our logistics operation
   can help minimize repair time and avoid
   unnecessary interference with your schedule.
SPARE PARTS
• Owners of Hawk lenses who perform their
   own service and repair can depend on Hawk
   for lifetime parts service.
• Hawk stocks a large inventory of spare parts
   for all lens series in Germany. This underground
   storage inventory is strategically tailored to
   minimize down time.
• If a needed part is unavailable, Hawk will
   manufacture it according to our original
   drawings and specifications.
• Telephone consultation is available for
   identifying a part or a problem. Service and
   repair videos can be provided upon request.
• Express shipping is available anywhere in
   the world. Hawk’s logistics pros are experts
   on tariffs, customs and documents.
MAINTENANCE TRAINING
Hawk offers two-day in-person training for lens
technicians at the factory in Germany.
