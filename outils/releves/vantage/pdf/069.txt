NEW ADDITION:
THE MINIHAWK 29 MM T1.7
SUPER WIDE ANGLE LENS
The MiniHawk line of hybrid anamorphics now includes
an 80° super wide angle: the new 29 mm/T1.7 lens.
Like all MiniHawks, the 29 mm
comes with very close focus –
10” or 0.25 m in this case.
MiniHawks are spherical lenses
that use a patented shaping
device to produce classic
anamorphic characteristics
while eliminating many of
the inconveniencies – size,
weight, distortion, breathing –
that come with traditional
squeeze optics.

 STREAMLINED CG – The control and consistency of the MiniHawk spherical
 lenses offer greater convenience and efficiency in post and CG on today’s
 FX-heavy productions, while maintaining full-blown anamorphic flavor.
