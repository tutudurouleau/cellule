Hawk class-X
Classic 2x Anamorphic Lenses - Technical Data
Built on 30 years of lens design and manufacturing experience, Hawk class-X lenses
reflect the tremendous changes in filmmaking technology. This glass delivers impro-
ved definition, close focus and edge distortion while maintaining the organic look
and feel of all Hawk lenses. The Hawk class-X front anamorphic zoom lenses are
perfectly matched to the primes.
