Hawk C-Series
The very first lens line produced by Hawk - Technical Data
The very first lens line produced by Hawk. The Hawk C‑Series lenses deliver thoroughly modern and
sharp imagery that is also slightly forgiving, with a very natural feel. Designed with an understanding of
how filmmakers work, the C‑Series family includes a 60 mm/T 2.2 lens – a very useful focal length in the
2x format that had usually been left out in previous anamorphic lens sets. Because of their popularity,
Hawk continues to produce parts and maintain the C‑Series line.
