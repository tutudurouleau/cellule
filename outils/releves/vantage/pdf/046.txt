Hawk class-X Zooms
2x Front Anamorphic Zoom Lenses - Technical Data
Calculated from scratch, and built in a strictly classic design without resorting to nontraditional methods,
Hawk class‑X front anamorphic zoom lenses deliver improved definition, close focus and edge distortion
while maintaining the organic look and feel of all Hawk lenses. These zoom lenses are perfectly
matched to the Hawk class-X primes.
