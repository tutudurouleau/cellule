The Vantage Slender Diopter offers cinematographers the conveni-
ence of a high quality diopter in a standard rectangular 4x5.65“
filter frame. By using extremely expensive OharaTM S-LAH51 glass,
we’ve created an ultra thin tool that uses a single filter slot in a
standard ARRITM matte box – often an advantage given the increa-
sed need for filters in today’s digital cameras. The Vantage Slender
Diopter also obviates the need for time-consuming mounting with
a “donut.” Instead, adding or removing the Slender Diopter® is as
easy and quick as adding or removing a filter.

The Vantage Slender Diopter® has a magnification strength of +0.8,
as opposed to the normal +1, extending the focal distance at infinity,
with a small sacrifice of close focus distance. This compromise offers
additional flexibility at the far end of the focal plane.

Features of the Vantage Slender Diopter:
• Extremely thin – uses a single filter slot
• Rectangular shape covers wide lenses
• Magnification power of +0.8 adds distance
• Multiple anti-reflective coating on both sides eliminates ghosting
• Very convenient insertion and removal even with clip-on matte boxes
• Precise manufacturing yields extremely high picture quality with
  almost no distortion or aberration
• Designed to work seamlessly with Hawk and Vantage lenses
