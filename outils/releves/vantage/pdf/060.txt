Hawk V-Series
2x Anamorphic Lenses - Technical Data
An extensive range of high performance ‘Scope lenses. The V‑Series line has you covered, extending
superior lens quality into very long focal lengths, including an unprecedented 250/350 mm lens and
two additional zooms. At the other end of the scale, the V‑Series lenses deliver dramatic breakthroughs
in close focus – down to two feet in the middle range of focal lengths. This virtue is especially welcome
for cinematographers shooting ‘Scope in tight locations.
