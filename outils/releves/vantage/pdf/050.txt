Hawk V-Plus 1.3x Vintage’74 Zooms
Front Anamorphic Zoom Lenses - Technical Data
Genuine 1.3x front anamorphic zoom lenses. The Hawk V‑Plus 1.3x Vintage‘74 front anamorphic
zooms deliver the lower contrast and flare characteristics of 1970s lenses, combined with the distinctive
1.3x squeeze format. These zoom lenses intercut seamlessly with the entire line of Hawk V‑Lite 1.3x
Vintage’74 primes.
