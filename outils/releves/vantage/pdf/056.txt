Hawk V-Plus
2x Anamorphic Lenses - Technical Data
Fully matched, beautiful lenses designed to handle extreme high contrasts. Hawk V‑Plus anamorphics yield slightly
greater definition and subtly boosted contrast. Superior optical design is also evident in two super-close-focus lenses, the
65 mm and the 120 mm, which focus down to the front element. The V‑Plus line of lenses handles high contrast situations
exceptionally well, and features masking to eliminate extraneous light rays. Hawk V‑Plus lenses match perfectly with other
Hawk glass, imbuing images with all the subtle qualities cinematographers love in classic anamorphic photography.
