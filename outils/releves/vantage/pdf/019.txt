Hawk V‑Lite16 Vintage’74
1.3x Anamorphic Lenses - Technical Data
The Hawk V-Lite16 Vintage’74s incorporate a precision Iris Shaping System that delivers
classic 2x anamorphic bokeh. The Vintage’74 coating technology provides authentic period
signature, combined with modern, reliable mechanics and contemporary optics. Like the
regular Hawk V-Lite 16s, they offer excellent T-stop range and the shallow depth of field
