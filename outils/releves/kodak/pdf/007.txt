Developer Replenisher RT. In one case, you can replace the
existing developer tank solution with a fresh tank mix—see

  Use of Activated-Carbon Filters to Remove Stain—
  In roller-transport processors, a build-up of oxidized
  chemicals in the developer tank solution can stain prints. To
  remove these oxidized chemicals and prevent staining,
  install an activated-carbon filter. You will see a significant
  stain reduction within 24 hours. The life of the filter
  cartridge depends on the amount of oxidation products that
  are treated and the size of the processor tank; however,
  typical length of use is about 14 days with a 75 litre
  (20-gallon) tank.
