9. Hold the front of the jib to compensate for any shift in weight and carefully remove the front

Note: To avoid the sections jamming, after using the jib and the camera (weights etc
already removed), always shorten the front of the arm first, then adjust the rear
section. When adjusting the length of the arm, remove the camera!
